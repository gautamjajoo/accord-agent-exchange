## Deliberate demo limits

- All merchants, products and institutions are real references; campaigns, discounts, consumer integrations and marked base-price quotes are fictional. Discount codes are not redeemable.
- Stripe evidence is sandbox transfer evidence, not live-money settlement or bank payouts. The shared presentation stage is funded; fresh private workspaces start with zero sandbox balance.
- Earlier delayed Checkout funding events required official Stripe resends. A fresh later payment verified automatic signed `charge.updated` reconciliation after the fee-timing fix; the observed failure and correction are retained in Stripe verification documentation.
- GPT decisions vary. Recorded live replay preserves a reproducible lower-CPC winner; fresh auctions are not forced to select it. A legitimate one-cent CPC produced a zero-cent publisher share under integer rounding and correctly required no transfer.
- No coupon redemption, stacking, separate eligibility/fraud system, live inventory availability guarantee, or production financial operations are included, matching the agreed boundaries.

No unresolved implementation or external-account blocker remains in the inspected acceptance evidence. Completion of the overall goal is the parent’s decision based on this audit, its final terminal receipts and browser verification.
